**Effective Date:** October 22, 2026  
**Last Updated:** October 22, 2026  
**Publisher / Developer:** Solid Take Games  
**Contact Email:** Mikel.bryan97@gmail.com  

Solid Take Games ("we," "our," or "us") is dedicated to protecting the privacy of our players. This Privacy Policy outlines how your information is handled when you download, install, and play **Orbital Slice: Space Roguelite** ("the Game") on Android devices via Google Play.

---

### 1. Information We Collect
**Orbital Slice: Space Roguelite** does not require user registration, log-ins, or account creation. We do not collect or store personal identifiable information (such as your legal name, physical address, email address, or phone number).

#### A. Automated Gameplay Data & Offline Storage
All core gameplay progression data, including unlocked ship hulls, high scores, Talent Lab upgrades, and resonance core balances, is stored strictly locally on your device via standard local storage.

#### B. Advertising and Analytics Data
To support free-to-play features and optional rewarded revival telemetry, the Game integrates the **Google Mobile Ads SDK (Google AdMob)**. Google AdMob may collect and process device-specific information, including:
- Google Advertising ID (AAID)
- Device model, manufacturer, and operating system version
- Network carrier and IP address
- Ad interaction events, performance metrics, and coarse diagnostic telemetry

### 3. Children’s Privacy
The Game is compliant with COPPA (Children's Online Privacy Protection Act) and Google Play Families policies. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. Ad requests sent through AdMob are flagged appropriately according to applicable child-directed regulations where required.

---

### 4. Data Security
We implement standard, reasonable technical safeguards to ensure local and transmitted telemetry remains secure and protected against unauthorized access.

---

### 5. Your Rights and Opt-Out Options
You can reset or opt out of personalized ads at any time via your Android device settings: *Settings > Google > Ads > Delete advertising ID* or *Reset advertising ID*.
